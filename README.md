### Hi, I’m CJ Jarmaine ⚡

**Sparky & Electrical Engineering student** based in Australia, exploring the intersection of hardware, embedded systems, and software.

I’m interested in understanding how systems work from the physical layer upward—circuits, microcontrollers, power, signals, and the code that brings them to life.

---

### ⚡ Background & Focus

I work as an electrician and study Electrical Engineering, building a strong foundation across both sides of the stack:

* **Hardware:** Circuits, wiring, power, sensors, PCBs, and test equipment
* **Firmware & Embedded:** Microcontrollers (ESP32, STM32), actuators, and real-world control
* **Software & Infrastructure:** Python, C/C++, Linux, Proxmox, Git, and automation
* **Documentation:** Clear READMEs, build logs, wiring diagrams, and reproducible setups

Right now, I am focused on turning project ideas into documented, reusable repositories with clear schematics, setup instructions, and lessons learned.
